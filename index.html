About me

I live and work in Bologna. I have experience as an assistant editor, editor, colorist, QC and mastering.

Since 2022 I have been part of the Silly Monkeys collective, where I take care of post-production for our work.

Since 2024 I have been working at KoreLab Srl , where I work as an assistant editor and post-production assistant, handling deliveries and QC, collaborating with national and international productions.
